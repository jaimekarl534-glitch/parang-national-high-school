Dance troupe wins 3rd runner-up in Iloilo

Achievements · Jan 23, 2026

The PNHS Passionate Dancerists placed third runner-up at the ILOmination: Festival of Light Festivals 2026 in Iloilo City.

The Oriental Mindoro provincial government shared the result and said Mindoro youth showed their talents at major festivals and competitions around the country.

About our school

We are a public secondary school in Parang that welcomes every learner, regardless of background, and helps them grow in knowledge, skills and values.

Our teachers work closely with parents and the local community so students are supported in class and at home.

Vision

Responsible, innovative and future-ready Filipino learners who contribute to their communities and the nation.

Mission

To provide quality, inclusive and learner-centered education in a safe and caring environment.

Core values

Excellence, character and service.

Academic programs

Junior High School

Senior High School
